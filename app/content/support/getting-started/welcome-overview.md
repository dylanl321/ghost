# Welcome to GHOST

This short overview helps you get oriented and productive quickly.

## What you can do in GHOST

- Universal Search: Find accounts, users, and resources instantly from anywhere.
- Cost Dashboard: Monitor spend, analyze trends, and export/share cost views.
- Organization Explorer: Navigate your org, environments, and accounts.
- Reports: Schedule one-time or recurring cost reports.

## Cost Dashboard highlights

- Switch between Billable and Proforma views.
- Choose Timeframe and View presets for quick analysis.
- Use the Accounts selector to narrow focus.
- Explore charts and breakdowns; export to PDF/CSV/Excel.
- Generate a shareable link with your current view settings.
